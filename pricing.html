Taxi van vs regular cab

When five or more of you are travelling, one van on one flat fare beats juggling multiple cabs.

Auckland taxi van Regular cab
Seats Up to 11 passengers in one vehicle Up to 4 passengers
Luggage Room for the whole group's suitcases, prams and sports gear A standard car boot
Pricing One flat fare per van, not per person. Split it any way you like A separate fare for every cab your group fills
The group Everyone travels together, one pickup and one arrival The group splits up and regroups at the other end

The maths for 5 people, Auckland CBD to Auckland Airport: one taxi van is a flat $97, under $20 each. The same group in two separate cabs pays $65 per cab, $130 all up. The van saves $33 and keeps everyone together.
